## Outcome Sought

Translate management support for productization into named engineering and
pilot ownership with enough protected time to reduce the single-maintainer
risk.

## Why Now

Organizational permission is no longer the main uncertainty. Execution
capacity, backup ownership, and support expectations remain unresolved.

## Scope

- Name one accountable VIR maintainer and one backup maintainer.
- Name one user owner and one VIR owner for the Lake/browser pilot.
- Name one reviewer for the binding and lifecycle contract.
- Agree on protected capacity for release, CI, support-contract, and pilot
  work.
- Agree on escalation and six-week review points.
- Do not assign people without their explicit commitment.

## Done When

- The maintainer, backup, pilot owners, and binding/lifecycle reviewer are
  named, with overlaps made explicit.
- Each person accepts the responsibility and expected time window.
- The release and pilot cards have accountable owners.
- The maintenance document records the operating agreement.
- Any unfilled role is recorded as a pilot blocker rather than silently
  absorbed by the primary maintainer.

## Dependencies

- Management sponsor and candidate owners are available for confirmation.

## Evidence

Management support for productization was confirmed in the review discussion;
specific owners and capacity have not yet been recorded.
