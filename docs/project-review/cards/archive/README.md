# Action Card Archive

Move a card here under `archive/<year>/` after its acceptance criteria pass,
or after an explicit consolidation makes it superseded. Its closure section
must record:

- the result;
- dated evidence;
- unexpected findings;
- explicit follow-up cards; and
- the living documents updated with durable conclusions.

A superseded card must name the active card that now owns its unfinished
outcome. Superseding a card is not evidence that its acceptance criteria
passed.

Archived cards are execution history. They are not the source of truth for the
current product boundary, upstream strategy, maintenance policy, or roadmap.
